Dec 2026 KPCT→Jebel Ali consignment కి Force Majeure probability తక్కువ. కానీ Red Sea Houthi risk, cyclone season (Oct-Dec), port strike అప్పుడప్పుడు వస్తుంది. LC లో FM clause, Insurance ICC-A, Maersk rebooking protocol — ముందే ready ఉండాలి.
🌪️
Force Majeure అంటే ఏమిటి? — ఎవరి నియంత్రణలో లేని అనివార్య పరిస్థితి
Force Majeure = ఎవరి control లో లేని అనివార్య సంఘటనలు. Vessel delay, port strike, cyclone, war, pandemic — ఇవన్నీ FM events. FM invoke చేస్తే — penalty లేదు. కానీ documentary evidence తప్పనిసరి. LC amendment కావాలి. Insurance claim trigger కావాలి.
🚢 Container: MRKU3456789 · Maersk · KPCT→Jebel Ali · Dec 2026
🌡️ Reefer: 13°C ±1°C · 18-day transit · Cold chain critical
📋 FEED MoU Common Clause #9: Force Majeure pre-loaded
💡 FM = LC void కాదు — amendment required only
📜
ఒప్పందంలో Force Majeure నిబంధన — ముఖ్యమైన విషయం
"Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations under this contract if such failure is caused by events beyond the reasonable control of that party, including but not limited to: natural disasters, war, civil war, strikes, government restrictions, pandemic port closures, or currency convertibility restrictions."
⚠️ కీలక సత్యం: FM = LC automatically void కాదు. LC validity extension కోసం buyer consent + bank amendment అవసరం. FM invocation ఆటోమేటిక్ కాదు — documentary proof + notification mandatory.

Pandemic / Port Closure (COVID-type)
Port closure by government order. Payment delay అయినా penalty లేదు — documentary evidence తో FM invoke చేయవచ్చు. COVID-19 లాంటి situations కి FM clause protect చేస్తుంది.
⚡ FM invoke: documentary evidence + APEDA/DGFT FM certificate + buyer notification within 24 hours.

📋
సమస్య వచ్చినప్పుడు చేయాల్సిన 5 దశలు
1
⏰ Day 0 — వెంటనే
📢 Buyer (Al Barakat) Notification
FM event confirm అయిన వెంటనే Al Barakat కి WhatsApp + Email: "Force Majeure event occurred — [Cyclone Vardah / Houthi attack / Port Strike]. Shipment delayed. Initiating LC extension request. Evidence documents shared within 48 hours."
✅ 24 గంటలలోపు notify — delay = contractual default risk
2
⏰ Day 1-3 — LC Extension Request
🏦 Buyer Consent → LC Amendment
Al Barakat consent తో: Al Barakat bank (UAE issuing bank) కి LC validity extension instruction. LC amendment cost: $50-100. Processing: 5-7 banking days. UCP 600 Art 10: LC amendment = issuing bank + confirming bank + FEED consent. FM = automatic extension కాదు — buyer consent mandatory.
💰 Amendment fee: $50-100 per bank · 5-7 banking days processing
3
⏰ Day 1-2 — Insurance Trigger
🛡️ ICC-A Insurance Claim Trigger
IFFCO Tokio ICC-A + IRMC (Reefer Machinery) policy: FM transit delay damage cover. Notice within 24 hours of loss discovery mandatory. Joint survey appointment. Banana reefer: cold chain breach during FM delay = ICC-A + IRMC covers temperature excursion damage.
📞 IFFCO Tokio: 1800-103-5499 · Notice within 24 hours
4
⏰ Day 2-5 — Alternative Logistics
🔄 Maersk Alternative Vessel Booking
Vessel breakdown: Maersk 1800-102-2477 — alternative vessel request. KPCT closed: Vizag (VCTPL) alternative port use. Maersk rebooking: 5-7 days typical. Reefer power continuity: ensure alternative vessel has reefer plug. MRKU3456789 container transfer protocol.
🚢 Alternative port: Vizag VCTPL if KPCT closed · reefer plug continuity
5
⏰ Day 2-7 — Government Certification
📜 APEDA/DGFT Force Majeure Certificate
FM documentary evidence: (1) Maersk vessel delay notification (2) IMD cyclone alert (official) (3) Port authority strike notice (4) News publications (official) + APEDA/DGFT FM certificate issue. ECGC notification: if payment delay due to FM — ECGC inform (30-day rule still applies).
📋 DGFT Vizag Ashwini Ji: 0891-2746641 · FM certificate request
⚔️
Red Sea / Houthi ప్రమాదం — Jebel Ali మార్గంలో రైతు తెలుసుకోవాల్సింది
KPCT→Jebel Ali route: direct Indian Ocean route (NOT Red Sea route). 18-day transit Houthi risk తక్కువ. Gulf of Aden minor risk only.
Red Sea risk mainly EU route (KPCT→Rotterdam). Dec 2026 Jebel Ali consignment → relatively safer corridor.
War Risk insurance addendum: IFFCO Tokio policy లో separate War Clauses addendum — Gulf of Aden piracy coverage. Additional premium: 0.05-0.3% insured value.
LC buffer rule: LC expiry date = vessel departure date + 10 days minimum buffer. Red Sea detour వస్తే: LC amendment ready.

రైతు తప్పక నివారించాల్సిన పొరపాట్లు
✖ Force Majeure = LC automatically cancel అవుతుందని think చేయడం. FM = extension request మాత్రమే. Buyer consent mandatory.
✖ FM invoke చేసి ECGC 30-day notification miss చేయడం — FM event అయినా ECGC notification rule change కాదు.
✖ "Insurance అన్నీ pays చేస్తుంది" అని guarantee చేయడం — ICC-A covers transit damage only. Payment default కాదు.
✖ Reefer power cut during port hold ignore చేయడం — temperature log every 30 min. IRMC claim documentation start చేయాలి.
✖ FM documentary evidence లేకుండా invoke చేయడం — IMD official alert, port authority notice, Maersk notification — అన్నీ required.
