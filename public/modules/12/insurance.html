🚨 Critical Distinction — ECGC ≠ Marine Insurance
ECGC (Export Credit Guarantee) = CREDIT RISK = Buyer payment default / country risk. Marine Cargo Insurance = CARGO RISK = physical damage or loss during transit. రెండూ వేర్వేరు risks — రెండూ వేర్వేరు insurers. Confuse చేస్తే — wrong claim file అవుతుంది, payment రాదు.
⚡ FEED కి రెండూ mandatory: Marine (IFFCO Tokio) + ECGC (Anand Singh).

🚢 Marine Cargo Claim — IFFCO Tokio ICC-A 6 Documents · 60-90 Days
1
⏰ Loss discovery వెంటనే
📢 IFFCO Tokio ని Notice చేయడం
Cargo damage / reefer breakdown / theft — discover అయిన వెంటనే 24 గంటలలో IFFCO Tokio claim helpline call చేయాలి. Policy number, BL number, loss nature చెప్పాలి. Notice delay = claim rejection risk.
⏰ 24 గంటలలో notice mandatory · IFFCO Tokio: 1800-103-5499
2
⏰ Day 1-2
🔍 Joint Survey Appointment
IFFCO Tokio appointed surveyor + FEED representative — container/cargo joint inspection. SGS / Cotecna / Bureau Veritas UAE లో appointing అవుతారు. Survey report = claim basis. Damage photos before + after తీయాలి. Temperature log data logger download.
📸 Photos mandatory: before damage + after damage + packaging condition
3
⏰ Day 3-7
📋 Claim Letter Submit
Formal claim letter: policy number + BL number + invoice number + nature of loss + estimated value. 6-document bundle compile చేసి IFFCO Tokio కి submit. Carrier's Note of Protest (vessel/shipping line నుంచి) — delay damage కి evidence.
📄 Carrier's Note of Protest = shipping line నుంచి తీసుకోవాలి
4
⏰ Day 7-90
✅ Assessment & Settlement
IFFCO Tokio claim assessor survey report review చేస్తాడు. Loss quantum determine చేస్తాడు. ICC-A all risks coverage confirm. Settlement: 60-90 రోజులలో. Contested claims: ICC arbitration (London). Bank statement of settlement — loss vs total shipment ratio.
⏳ Settlement: 60-90 రోజులు · Contested: ICC London arbitration

🏦 ECGC WTP Claim — Buyer Default 8 Documents · 4-6 Months
1
⚡ Immediate ECGC Notification
Al Barakat payment due date మించిన వెంటనే 30 రోజులలో ECGC Hyderabad notify చేయాలి. Anand Singh GM: +91-8373912942 · ecgchyd@ecgc.in.
⏰ Payment due + 30 రోజులు · MISS = Claim FORFEIT
2
⏳ Waiting Period
Commercial Risk: 4 నెలలు వేచి ఉండాలి. ఆ వ్యవధిలో payment రాకపోతే claim file. Political Risk: 3 నెలలు. ఈ period లో FEED recovery attempts continue చేయాలి.
📅 Commercial: 4 నెలలు · Political: 3 నెలలు
3
📂 Claim Documents Submit (8 Bundle)
Complete 8-document bundle submit: కింద grid లో చూడండి. Documents incomplete = claim reject.
🗂️ 8 documents complete bundle mandatory
4
🔍 ECGC Investigation
ECGC claim investigation team. UAE correspondent bank reports. Al Barakat financial status + country risk UAE (GREEN) verify. ECGC may directly approach Al Barakat for payment.
⏳ Total timeline: 4-6 నెలలు
5
✅ Settlement — 60-90% Loss Coverage
Approve అయితే: 60-90% FOB coverage. Balance 10-40% FEED/farmer భరిస్తారు. Payment: 30 రోజులలో. ECGC తర్వాత Al Barakat పై legal action తీసుకుంటుంది.
💰 60-90% FOB · Balance farmer / FEED · NEVER 100%
